This week saw the addition of 16 new records to the
[Semantic Farm](https://semantic.farm) and the
[Bioregistry](https://bioregistry.io) for ontologies, controlled vocabularies,
and schemas relevant to chemistry that were either presented or discussed at the
[5<sup>th</sup> Ontologies4Chem Workshop](https://nfdi4chem.de/event/5th-ontologies4chem-workshop/)
in Limburg an der Lahn from November 2<sup>nd</sup>-4<sup>th</sup>, 2026 along
with an accompanying [collection](https://semantic.farm/collection/0000047)
covering these discussions.
